The V2 standard

A good alert tells the person who receives it what broke, how bad it is, and what to do. It is sent twice: once when it fires and once when it clears.

Needed to finish phase 1

Say what failed, where, and by how much

The message alone should be enough to know what is wrong.

Not thisError OccurredThisWarehouse load lag above 30m for 15m

Report a problem, never a status

If nobody has to do anything, it is a log line. Delete the alert.

Not thisi am aliveThisNo alert at all

Use real names

Application and component name a real thing someone can find.

Not thisDefaultThisstream-router

Fire once, clear once

No alert stays on for days or flips on and off. Three or more fire-and-clear cycles in 24 hours is flapping. V2 has no expiry time: polled alerts are cleared by their collector, not by a timer.

Not this41 incidents in a weekThisFires, gets handled, clears

Severity passes the wake-up test

Critical means urgent, causing damage right now, and someone can act on it. Otherwise it is lower.

Not thisCritical for a nightly report delayThisWarning, handled in working hours

Don't send what your own dashboard hides

If every panel you own filters an alert out, fix or delete it at the source.
